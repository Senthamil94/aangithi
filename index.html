Our story

Angithi — the humble clay stove every Indian home once cooked on

We named our kitchen after it because that's the food we make: honest, slow, homestyle North Indian cooking. Every dal is tempered fresh, every naan finished with butter, every gravy built from whole spices we roast ourselves.

Good to know

Questions, answered

Is Aangithi fully vegetarian?

No — the dining menu includes both vegetarian and non-vegetarian dishes. Green marks vegetarian items and red marks non-vegetarian. Vegetarian catering trays are listed separately on our Catering page.

Do you offer delivery in Morgan Hill?

Yes. Choose Delivery when you order online and enter your address to check availability. Pickup is typically ready in 15–20 minutes.

Can I schedule an order for later?

Absolutely. Pick "Schedule for Later" at checkout and choose a date and 15-minute time slot.

Do you cater parties?

Yes. Browse our Catering page for trays, breads and party appetizers. Catering hours are 9:00 AM – 10:00 PM PT, with pickup or delivery.
