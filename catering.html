Good to know

Catering questions

How do I place a catering order?

Tap Order Catering to open our catering checkout. Choose pickup or delivery, add trays, then schedule a time between 9:00 AM and 10:00 PM PT.

What sizes do trays come in?

Most entrees are 16 oz (about 2 people), half tray (about 18 people) and large tray (about 50 people). Rice singles are 32 oz. Some appetizers and breads are sold per serving or per piece.

Do you deliver catering in Morgan Hill?

Yes. Select Delivery at checkout and enter your address to check availability. Pickup is at 15862 Monterey Rd.

Is everything vegetarian?

Our entire kitchen is 100% vegetarian. No meat, fish or eggs are used anywhere.
